# Sky panoramas

Daytime skies use two CC0 panoramas from Poly Haven. Both have been resized and encoded as WebP for this game.

- [Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky)
- [Qwantani Late Afternoon (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/qwantani_late_afternoon_puresky)
