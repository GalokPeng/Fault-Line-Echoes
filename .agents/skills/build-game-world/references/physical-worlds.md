# Worlds that hold together

Start from the decision the terrain creates. If players choose a ford for speed or a high bank for safety, place both approaches, one reason to commit and a believable way back before decorating. For another genre the same test could be a freight yard with two switchbacks or a climbing puzzle with two handholds.

**One coordinate contract.** Agree on units, world axes, origin and simulation time. Expose shared queries such as ground elevation, walkable support, wet/depth/current, reachability and occlusion. Terrain mesh, collider, NPC footing, map marks, sound and objects should answer from the same data. Walk the route at ankle height. At bridges, tunnels or balconies, distinguish actual support from the terrain underneath. Put spawn and gate arrival points on the traversable side, outside automatic trigger volumes.

**Mountains, settlements and water.** Lay out drainage basins, ridges, saddles and roads. Grade a buildable footprint using corners and access paths, then fit foundations and stairs; a single centre-height sample can bury the downhill door. Carve a riverbed and bank cross-sections, interpolate a downstream water surface and clip it at the shoreline. Derive flow direction from the channel, steer around obstacles, render moving detail along that field. Make lakes a separate still-water case. Floating props use the same field, drift until caught or stranded at a recoverable bank, save their position and ownership. Test uphill banks, isolated pools, dropping and relaunching, and a player looking up from below a bridge.

**Human and wildlife movement.** Define job and home locations, paths and time windows. Give jobs a physical workstation, an interaction point in front of it and an action that lines up with the tools. Track a moving giver's current location. For perception, combine facing, line of sight, surface noise, action and distance. Hearing locates the noise source; searching and pursuit have an end condition and a return path. Distinguish an owned item, a witnessed taking and a gift. On death/harvest, tie each item to the species, capacity and inventory transaction. Reserve rare items for identifiable body parts or behaviours. Schedule day and night populations and keep markers in sync.

**What to run.** Walk the spawn-to-exit route and return through the gate; enter a workshop, climb a measured ledge and fail an over-height climb. Sample camera/feet/water/collider height on each region's shore. Drop an item into moving water and recover the same inventory identity once. Approach a creature quietly outside its view, make noise on a different surface, then escape its chase radius. Test these only where promised by the chosen design.
